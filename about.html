About This Blog

Thinking Clearly is a blog about the art and science of making better decisions. We explore mental models, cognitive biases, investment psychology, and practical frameworks that help you think more clearly in a noisy world.

The ideas here draw from a wide range of disciplines: behavioral economics, value investing, Stoic philosophy, systems thinking, and cognitive science. We believe that decision-making is a skill that can be practiced and improved, not just an innate talent.

Why Decision-Making Matters

Every aspect of your life is shaped by the decisions you make. Your career, finances, relationships, and health all depend on the quality of your judgment. Yet most of us never formally study how to make better choices. We rely on intuition, habit, and social proof without questioning whether those defaults serve us well.

This blog aims to change that. Each article provides a specific concept, framework, or technique you can apply immediately. No jargon, no academic abstractions - just clear thinking tools for real-world decisions.
